06 / FAQ

Clear answers,
before you begin.

Will Chameleon invent experience or qualifications?+

No. Tailoring is constrained to wording, ordering, and emphasis supported by the master resume. Companies, titles, dates, locations, and metrics are preserved.

What can I pass into the tailor command?+

A job posting URL, pasted text, a text-file path, or standard input. The workflow extracts the role signal and saves a reusable JSON analysis locally.

Can I use the same job analysis for later documents?+

Yes. A saved analysis can be reused to tailor another CV, score a version, generate a cover letter, or prepare a screening-question response.

Where can I read the full documentation?+

The documentation links below open GitHub’s rendered Markdown view, rather than the plain source file.
